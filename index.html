What can we book today?+

Speak with us about wedding photography and films, pre-wedding shoots, portraits, special celebrations and drone coverage. Availability and coverage are confirmed with the team.

How do we request a quote or callback?+

Use “Get a callback” or the enquiry form. Both prepare a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. Your request is confirmed by the team, not automatically booked through the website.

Are the smart gallery features live?+

Not yet. Every feature marked “Coming up next” is planned. Client login, face recognition, guest uploads and private galleries are not active on this site.

How will Kwikpic fit into our experience?+

We plan to use Kwikpic for photo galleries and related discovery and sharing tools. Access links, account steps, privacy settings and supported features will be explained after setup. No account connection or photo upload is required here today.

How will online photo access work?+

Online photo access will follow the selected Kwikpic plan’s storage allowance, permissions and access terms. We’ll explain the applicable limits before activating your gallery.

When will our photos, films and albums be ready?+

We’ll agree delivery milestones for your booking. The site does not promise a fixed turnaround. Album and print ordering are marked “Coming up next” until the service is ready.

What about pricing, changes and cancellation?+

Your quote will set out coverage, fees, travel, payment milestones, revisions and cancellation terms. Please discuss changes directly with the team before confirming your booking.
